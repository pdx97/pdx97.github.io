Prakhar Dixit

PhD Student, Computer Science
University of Maryland, Baltimore County

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC). My research asks how AI systems reason, learn from experience, and use memory.

Most of my current work is on mathematical reasoning in large language models and on memory for LLM agents: how an agent can accumulate reusable problem-solving strategies over time (ISM), and why agents sometimes confidently misremember their own past (Honest Lying). Earlier, I worked on sample efficiency in reinforcement learning and on sim-to-real transfer for vision-based drone navigation.

In summer 2026 I was an Applied Scientist Intern at Microsoft, working on agent skills in Copilot Studio. Before that I was a research intern at Emergence AI, working on long-term memory for conversational agents.

Research Interests
